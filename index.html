    Questions

    Good to know.

    What actually comes in a box?

    250 g of marinated chicken thigh, diced fresh tomatoes, crisp onions and our signature Minuit sauce — plus your side, if you choose one. That base is identical in every box on the menu.

    What's in the Minuit sauce?

    A creamy, tangy mayo-based blend that we make ourselves. The exact recipe stays in the kitchen, but it's the reason most people order a second box.

    What if I don't want a side?

    Order the Simple Chicken Box. Chicken, vegetables and sauce, nothing else — it's the lightest option and the purest version of what we make.

    Do you deliver to me?

    Send us your address on WhatsApp when you place the order and we'll confirm straight away whether you're in range and how long it'll take.

    Can you cater a group or an event?

    Yes. The box scales cleanly for parties, offices, festivals and private events. Message us with your headcount and date and we'll put a quote together.
